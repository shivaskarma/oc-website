The ORBIT Model

From stillness to transformation

Five stages. From autopilot to awareness. From conditioning to clarity. From stuck to free.

O

Observe

Step back from the momentum.

Pause the momentum. Watch your mind without becoming it. The first step is not to fix, but to stop and look — discovering the space between stimulus and response where all transformation begins.

R

Recognise

Separate the pattern from the person.

See the pattern — the conditioning, the fear, the inherited script — as separate from you. Without the distortions of conditioning, you begin to perceive the stories you repeat, the beliefs you cling to. Recognition is the first crack in the wall.

B

Break Free

Understanding dissolves grip.

The pattern loses its grip not through force, but because it was seen clearly. Awareness dissolves what willpower cannot. You don't fight the old orbit — you simply stop feeding it.

I

Integrate

Insight without action is just entertainment.

Let the new understanding settle into your body, your relationships, your decisions. This is not theory — it is lived experience. Together we design actions that emerge from clarity, not from should.

T

Transform

A new trajectory, not a new trick.

Act from clarity, not conditioning. When perception is undistorted, right action flows naturally. The same situations that once triggered reactivity now meet a different you — grounded, perceptive, free.
